## Design Specificity Verdict

**Authored surface, borrowed skeleton — roughly 35% specific.**

Genuinely authored: the palette and type system (forest/oat/gold + Playfair + Cormorant for the money figure — not default fintech blue-and-Inter); the hero lighting treatment; the editorial detail language (the eyebrow rule, the 1px-gutter grid where the border shows *through* the gaps, the bled-off quote glyph); the regulatory footer with real IČO, address, tied-agent disclosure and a live ČNB register link.

Generic: everything between hero and footer. Split hero → stat strip → story + pull-quote → 4 numbered service tiles → 4-step numbered process → 3-card blog row → dark CTA banner → 3-column footer. That is the stock 2023 consultant landing page, section for section. Swap the CSS variables and the Czech copy and it's a dentist, an architect, or a law firm. The three stock blog images (beach sunset, corporate handshake, empty wallet) actively undo the hero's work.

The one asset that is unmistakably his — the Jemnice/Vysočina story — gets a half-width text block on screen three, while a fabricated number gets the largest type on screen two.

## Overall Impression

The hero is designed; the rest is assembled. There is real craft here — the portrait lighting, the story copy, the mobile calculator wizard, the regulatory footer — sitting inside an information architecture that works against the business. Four of five nav tabs are calculators, ten of twelve home-page links route away from booking, and on a phone there are **5.4 screens between the two conversion opportunities** with no phone number in between.

The single biggest opportunity is not visual. It is that this page is currently a calculator site with a portrait on it, and it should be an advisor's site with calculators in it.

## What's Working

**The hero portrait treatment.** A key light placed behind and above the figure, a separate contact shadow at his feet, a drop-shadow offset to be physically consistent with that light, and a mask that dissolves the suit into the frame instead of ending on a cut line — then a staggered arrival with a reduced-motion opt-out. On a page whose job is "trust this human", this is the highest-leverage decision on the site.

**The story block.** *"Začínal jsem jako bankéř a viděl jsem zevnitř, jak finanční instituce navrhují produkty primárně pro vlastní zisk."* Specific, checkable, slightly self-incriminating — which is why it works. Paired with the Jemnice quote it turns the obvious objection (why a small-town advisor?) into the reason to choose him.

**The mobile calculator wizard.** One question per screen, step counter, progress bar, 28px slider thumb, 16px inputs to defeat iOS zoom, 44px hit targets, live result staying visible below. Real progressive disclosure at exactly the moment cognitive load peaks.

## Priority Issues

**[P0] The "live" AUM figure is fabricated.**
The largest number on the site — 150 897 532 Kč, beside a pulsing green dot labelled "Aktualizováno živě" — is generated by a seeded PRNG drifting ±1.5% around a hardcoded constant. The tooltip concedes "simulováno dle tržních trendů", but the tooltip is a tap target most visitors never open.
*Why it matters:* three compounding harms. Credibility — the diligent visitor who opens that tooltip discards every *true* claim on the page, including the footer's real ones. Regulatory — Ondřej is a tied agent under ČNB supervision and the page links the ČNB register two screens below; presenting a simulated asset figure as live client AUM is a claim, not a design choice. Positional — a fabricated number is doing the page's primary persuasion work in the second viewport.
*Fix:* delete it, or replace with a verifiable claim at the same visual weight ("100+ klientů na Vysočině", "Srovnávám nabídky 15+ bank", "X let praxe") and drop the green dot and the word "živě" entirely.
*Command:* `/impeccable clarify`

**[P0] No conversion path on mobile between the hero and the footer.**
Home page is 6 548px at 375px wide. Hero CTA ends at 946px; the next booking CTA is at 5 328px; the only phone number on the entire site is at 5 683px. The component built to fix this — `.nc-sticky-bar` — is killed by `@media(max-width:767px){…display:none!important}` at line 858, under a comment claiming a replacement is active. There is no replacement: runtime check finds **zero fixed-position elements on any page at 375px**. The bar survives only in the 768–900px tablet sliver.
*Why it matters:* the largest measurable leak on the site. Interest peaks in the middle (story, process) and there is nothing there to act on. For a local advisor the highest-intent mobile action is a phone call, and it's buried at 87% scroll depth.
*Fix:* remove the override, extend the bar to all pages after the hero scrolls out, split it **[Zavolat] [Domluvit konzultaci]**.
*Command:* `/impeccable adapt`

**[P1] The 4-tab product IA buries the funnel and breaks the browser.**
Six of twelve home-page links lead into calculators; ten of twelve lead away from booking. The four service tiles all say "Otevřít kalkulačku →" — the section titled "Moje služby" is functionally a tool menu. `showPage()` never calls `pushState`, so all five tabs share one URL: Back exits the site and no calculator can be shared or bookmarked. The calculator pages themselves contain no portrait, no name, no trust content — the visitor who was warming to a person is now alone with a spreadsheet, at the moment of highest intent.
*Fix:* collapse the four calculators to one nav entry, free the slots for persuasion, add `pushState`/`hashchange`, put a persistent identity bar on every calculator page, and pipe the calculated result into the booking form.
*Command:* `/impeccable shape`

**[P1] The popup is the weakest surface at the highest-stakes moment.**
No Esc, no X, no focus trap, no autofocus. `<label>` elements carry no `for` attribute. One `alert()` covers three fields; `abc` passes as an e-mail. No GDPR consent box despite collecting name + phone + e-mail and shipping a full `gdpr.html`. And `Promise.allSettled` reports success **even when both transports reject** — the lead is destroyed while the user is told they'll be contacted within 24 hours.
*Why it matters:* every conversion passes through this one surface, so every defect is multiplied by the whole funnel. The false success doesn't lower the conversion rate — it silently deletes already-converted leads while reporting them as wins.
*Fix:* never show success unless a transport resolved; on total failure surface the phone number and a mailto fallback. Add Esc/X/focus trap/autofocus, wire `for`/`id`, add inline validation and a GDPR line, put Ondřej's face in the popup header.
*Command:* `/impeccable harden`

**[P2] Proof density is near zero relative to the size of the ask.**
Excluding the footer, 6 548px of page offers two proof elements: an unattributed "100+" and the fabricated AUM. No testimonials — though `.testimonials` is **fully styled** at lines 216–227 with stars, blockquote, author and role, and has no matching markup anywhere in the body. Someone built the trust section and pulled it. No Google rating, no named client, no personal ČNB registration number, no qualification, no years in practice, and no partner ever named despite "& partneři". The fee question is never answered, leaving "bez provizních tlaků" in unresolved tension with "vázaným zástupcem sítě INSIA" 4 000px later.
*Command:* `/impeccable clarify`

## Persona Red Flags

**Jordan (first-timer):** "PRIVÁTNÍ BANKOVNICTVÍ" disqualifies him in two seconds — reads as a wealth tier with a minimum. Clicks the "Chytré financování bydlení" tile expecting to read about the service, lands in a calculator prefilled with **6 000 000 Kč** and **1 200 000 Kč akontace** — words and numbers far above a first-timer. "Akontace" has a tooltip; **"LTV: 80,0 % OK"** has none. Desktop inputs render as raw `6000000` with no thousands separator — he can't tell six million from sixty million. The number he actually needs — how much must I earn to qualify — is never mentioned.

**Riley (stress tester):** taps the AUM tooltip, reads "simulováno" under a pulsing "živě" dot, and writes off "100+" too. **Casey (distracted mobile):** the face is **below the fold** — first viewport is headline, paragraph, two buttons, then ~250px of empty green. Two of five products are physically off-screen; `.nav-inner` is 582px of content in a 375px viewport with the scrollbar hidden and no fade or hint. It hits the two highest-intent products and not the two that use a `<div>`.
